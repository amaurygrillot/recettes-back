# Seed photo credits

The development database carries a handful of recipes with cover photos, uploaded by
[`scripts/upload-recipe-photos.ps1`](../scripts/upload-recipe-photos.ps1). All images come from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) under free licences, and were downloaded at a width of 1280 px
(Chocolate Fondant at its original 1200 px). Search a file name on Commons to reach its page.

CC BY and CC BY-SA require crediting the author, so **if any of these photos ever appears on a public deployment, this
table is the credit**. CC0 and public-domain images need none. Photos are for development and tests; they are not
shipped with the repository (the images live outside it, in `recettes-photos`).

The first photo of a recipe is its main one.
